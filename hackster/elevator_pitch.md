# Elevator pitch 候補

Hackster の Elevator pitch 欄は1〜2文。カバーGIFの隣に出るので、
**GIFで見えないこと**を書くのが効く（＝「実寸である」「アームが食器を運ぶ」）。

## 本命（推奨）

> A table that shows your meal at real size before you order — point, hold three seconds,
> and a robot arm brings the matching utensil.

134 文字。驚き（実寸）→ 操作（指で3秒）→ 落ち（アームが食器を運ぶ）の順で、
読み終えたときに全体像が残る。GIF の3段構成とも一致する。

## 代替案

**A. 課題から入る（審査員向け・ビジネス性を立てる）**

> Menu photos never tell you how much food actually arrives. This table projects the dish
> at 1:1, takes the order with a fingertip, and hands you the right utensil.

153 文字。「なぜ作ったか」が先に立つので、コンテストの審査文脈では強い。

**B. 体験だけを言う（一般読者・拡散向け）**

> Point at your table to order. The dish appears at its true size, and a robot arm sets
> down chopsticks or a fork to match.

119 文字。専門用語ゼロ。ショート動画の主題と同じ立て方。

**C. 食品ロスを主役にする（SDG 賞を狙う場合）**

> Life-size dishes projected onto your table, so you order the right amount — then a robot
> arm delivers the matching utensil.

122 文字。"cut food waste" を前に出したいときはこれ。

## 口頭用（30秒）

> Menu photos tell you what a dish looks like, but never how much of it there is.
> So we over-order and throw food away.
>
> As Served projects the dish onto your table at its real size — millimetre accurate,
> and you can check it with a ruler. You pick the dish and the size by pointing at the
> table and holding for three seconds. No touchscreen, nothing for a guest to touch.
>
> When the order lands, a SO-101 arm picks up the utensil that matches what you ordered.
> Chopsticks for ramen, a fork for pasta.
>
> The projection layer alone is a few hundred dollars of hardware, and it turns
> "how much food is that?" from something you read into something you see.

約110語 / 読み上げ 35〜40秒。展示ブースでの説明用。
